Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026
Developer: Michel Gerges
Contact: mnaut.dev@gmail.com

Selfhaunt ("the app") is a single-player arcade game for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no servers of ours. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

Kept only on your phone. The app saves your best scores, your daily challenge results and streak, the coins you have collected, your chosen colours, your sound and vibration settings, and whether you bought "Remove ads". This stays on your device; we never receive it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional rewarded ad (watch it to continue after a ghost catches you) and ads between games. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • how you interact with ads and the app (for example app launches, taps and video views);
  • diagnostic information (for example app launch time and performance);
  • device identifiers, such as your Android advertising ID and app set ID.

Google states this data is encrypted in transit. Google's use of it is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): before any ad is requested, the app shows Google's consent form where the law requires it. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy choices in the app.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings.
  • Remove ads: a one-time purchase turns off the ads between games. The optional rewarded ad stays available, and is only shown if you choose to watch it.

Purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are handled by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We never see your payment details. The app only learns whether "Remove ads" has been bought, and remembers that on your device.

Sharing pictures

If you tap Share after a game, the app creates a picture of your game and hands it to your phone's share menu. Where it goes from there is your choice; the app does not upload it anywhere.

Children

Selfhaunt is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us and we will help with any request we can.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no data about you on our own systems, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app. For data collected by Google for advertising, use your advertising ID controls and the in-app Privacy choices, or see Google's policies linked above.

Security

The app does not transmit your game data. Ad traffic between the app and Google is encrypted by Google's SDK.

Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
