SELFHAUNT

Every run you finish comes back as a ghost.

Selfhaunt is a one-finger arcade game about outrunning yourself. Drag anywhere to steer your dot to the coin. The moment you grab it, the path you just took becomes a ghost that replays in every round after. Touch a ghost and the run is over.

Every game ends in its own glowing tangle of the paths you ran. There's a daily challenge with the same coins for everyone, and five looks to unlock as you play.
